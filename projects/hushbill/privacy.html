Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Hushbill (“the app”) is made by Amoriel Apps (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. It’s short, because the app is built so that we never receive any of it.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no cloud of ours.
  • We don’t collect anything: no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no crash reports.
  • You can move or delete all your data at any time in Settings › Your data.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the bills, subscriptions and expenses you add: names, amounts, dates, notes, reminders and payment history;
  • your one-tap presets;
  • your settings, such as currency, reminders, appearance and lock preferences.

The app doesn’t connect to your bank, UPI or any other account. It only knows what you type in. It stays on your phone and is deleted with the app.

Face ID and the app passcode

Face ID is handled entirely by iOS: the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded, never anything about your face. If you set an app passcode, only a salted, one-way hash of it is kept, in your iPhone’s Keychain, on this device only.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us. You can choose to hide amounts on the Lock Screen (Settings › Hide amounts in alerts), and turn reminders off in the app or in your iPhone’s settings.

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

To show widgets and to answer Siri, the app shares a short summary (what’s due next, your preset names and amounts) with iOS on your device, in storage only Hushbill and its widgets can read. When you speak to Siri, Apple processes your request under Apple’s privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us.

Moving to a new iPhone

If you make a transfer file, it’s encrypted with the password you choose (AES-256), and you decide where it goes (AirDrop, Files, iCloud Drive and so on). That service’s own privacy terms apply. We never see the file or the password. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a Mac, Apple’s backup includes the app’s data under your Apple settings.

Links

Help, Privacy and Terms in the app open these web pages, and Send feedback opens your email app. Those are the only times the app uses the internet.

Purchases

Hushbill is a paid app sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t collect any information from them (or from anyone).

Your rights

You’re always in control: see everything in the app, export it with Move to a new iPhone, and erase it with Delete all data or by deleting the app. Because we hold no data about you, there’s nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Questions? Email amorielapps@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above, and explain any important change in the app before it applies to you.

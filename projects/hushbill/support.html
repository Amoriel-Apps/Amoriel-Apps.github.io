Quick answers

Reminders aren’t arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Hushbill in your iPhone’s Settings › Notifications, and that the bill or subscription has a reminder set (it’s on its edit screen). Reminders are scheduled on your phone up to 60 days ahead, so open Hushbill now and then to keep them topped up. Focus modes can also hold them back.

Why did I get one alert for several bills?

If more than one bill (or subscription) is due on the same day, Hushbill sends one alert with the total. Turn this off in Settings › Notifications › Combine same-day reminders. Free trials always get their own alert.

I’m moving to a new iPhone. How do I keep my data?

If you set up the new iPhone from an encrypted iCloud or Mac backup, or with Quick Start, Hushbill comes across with everything in it. Otherwise, on the old iPhone go to Settings › Move to a new iPhone, pick a password and AirDrop the file. On the new iPhone, open the file from the same screen (“This is my new one”) and enter the password. Your app passcode stays on the old phone, so set it again.

I forgot the app passcode

On Hushbill’s lock screen, tap Forgot it? Unlock with your iPhone passcode. Then set a new one in Settings › Face ID & passcode.

I forgot the password for my transfer file

We can’t open it: the file is locked with your password and we never see it. Make a new file on the old iPhone with a password you’ll remember.

How do I log things with Siri?

Say “Log an expense in Hushbill”, or “Log Chai in Hushbill” for one of your presets. “What’s due in Hushbill?” reads out the next 7 days. You’ll find all the phrases, plus how to add widgets and use the Action button and Control Centre, in Settings › Siri & quick logging.

I logged something by mistake

Touch and hold the expense in the Other tab to delete it. If you logged it from the widget, you can also tap Undo on the message that appears. For bills, open the bill and tap Undo last payment.

Can I get a refund?

Hushbill is bought through Apple, so refunds are too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Your data › Delete all data erases everything in Hushbill on this iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. There’s nothing stored anywhere else for us to delete. See the Privacy Policy.
